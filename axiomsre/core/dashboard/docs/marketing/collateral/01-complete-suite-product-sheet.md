## Page 1: The 6 Pillars of the Sovereign Suite

ChurchCore replaces 5 to 7 fragmented software vendors with one beautifully integrated, highly secure cloud platform. Built on **Next.js 16**, **TypeScript**, and **Supabase PostgreSQL Row-Level Security**, ChurchCore guarantees unprecedented speed, reliability, and data sovereignty.

### 1. 🏛️ ChurchCore (Core ChMS & Ministry Operations)
*The operational beating heart of the local church.*
* **Family & Household Directory:** Comprehensive profiles, relationships, custody restrictions, and pastoral visit notes.
* **Child Safety Check-In Kiosks (CCM):** High-speed barcode/token generation, allergy alerts, and verified emergency pickup checks.
* **Worship Service Planning:** Song catalogs, 1-click chord transposition, setlists, and volunteer scheduling with automated SMS/email confirmations.
* **Native Double-Entry General Ledger:** True fund accounting (Tithes, Missions, Building Fund) with automated donor tax receipts.
* **Digital Giving Rails:** Frictionless web, mobile, and recurring giving powered by Stripe Connect (0.85% platform fee).

### 2. 🎓 ChurchCore-LMS (Discipleship & Ministry Training)
*Interactive learning engine for volunteer certification and congregational formation.*
* **Pedagogical Course Builder:** Video hosting, interactive quizzes, discussion boards, and assignment rubrics.
* **Progressive Web App (PWA):** Offline course playback and local sync for missions and rural ministries.
* **Credentialing & Gamification:** XP engagement ledgers, automated digital certificates with QR-code public verification.
* **Standards Compliance:** Full SCORM and IMS Global OneRoster interoperability.

### 3. 🏫 ChurchCore-Academy (K-12 & Higher-Ed SIS)
*Complete student information system for Christian academies, day-schools, and Bible colleges.*
* **Academic Lifecycle:** Terms, semesters, course catalogs, section capacities, and automated prerequisite chains.
* **Admissions & Enrollment:** Public digital application forms, document uploads, and enrollment conversions.
* **Faculty Command:** Real-time roll call, section gradebooks, and the "Faculty Shepherd" pastoral monitoring view.
* **Parent & Student Portals:** Verifiable PDF transcripts, report cards, and academic standing rules (Honors, Dean's List).

*Confidential electronic health record (EHR) and telehealth platform for Christian clinics and church pastoral teams.*
