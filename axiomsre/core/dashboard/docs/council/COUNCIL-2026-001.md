## 1. Context & Problem Statement
Over the past multi-year development cycle, six independent, specialized, high-performance software platforms have been engineered under the ChurchCore umbrella:
1. **ChurchCore (ChMS & Finance):** 133 tables, 121 routes, mature double-entry accounting and child safety kiosk.
2. **ChurchCore-LMS:** 72 tables, 85 routes, 46 ratified Council decisions, offline PWA, and SCORM/xAPI runtime.
3. **ChurchCore-Academy:** 152 tables, 105 routes, comprehensive SIS lifecycle from admissions to graduation audits.
4. **ChurchCoreCare:** 84 tables, clinical & pastoral EHR, deny-all RLS isolation, integrated telehealth.
5. **ChurchCore-Orthos:** 28 tables, 30 routes, Gemini AI governance auditor, volunteer safeguarding, and elder assemblies.
6. **FaithFullScholars:** 48 tables, 53 routes, theological faculty recruitment, confessional fit, and accreditation dossiers.

Historically, these applications were developed with siloed operational focuses. ChurchCore now requires an overarching executive operating system, unified commercial packaging, and constitutional governance to establish the enterprise as the premier global software provider for the faith-based world.

---

## 2. Decision & Executive Specifications

1. **Portfolio Synthesis & Cohesive Identity:**
   - The six platforms are officially recognized as the six pillars of the ChurchCore Sovereign Suite.
   - A unified identity strategy ("ChurchCore ID") will be established to allow users single sign-on across the ecosystem while preserving strict cryptographic tenant isolation.

2. **Commercial Packaging Tiers:**
   - Four distinct commercial bundles are ratified:
     1. *Local Church Complete (ChMS + LMS + Basic Orthos)*: $199–$499/mo + 0.85% giving take-rate.
     2. *Christian Academy & Day-School Cloud (Academy + LMS + Orthos)*: $4,500–$15,000/yr.
     3. *Pastoral & Clinical Wellness (Care + Meet)*: $59/mo per practitioner.
     4. *Denominational & Diocese Enterprise Agreement*: $50,000–$250,000/yr.

3. **Constitutional Invariant Enforcement:**
   - Strict adherence to the 7 Non-Negotiable Constitutional Invariants:
     - Absolute separation between pastoral counseling notes and church membership directories.
     - Mandated Row-Level Security (RLS) on all 517 PostgreSQL tables.
     - Dual-Tier AI Council deliberation for all future strategic pivots and schema changes.

## 4. Amendments Adopted

- **Amendment 1 (Counseling Wall):** Under no circumstance shall any marketing campaign advertise "view your counseling notes in your church member portal." Confidentiality between counselor and client must remain strictly non-leaking.
- **Amendment 2 (Migration Shield):** Every bundle tier must ship with automated CSV/JSON data ingestion tools for churches migrating from Planning Center, Breeze, and FACTS to reduce switching friction to zero.
