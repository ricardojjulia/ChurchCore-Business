## 1. Executive Purpose: Building Generational Ministry Credibility

In secular technology, companies establish advisory boards of venture capitalists and corporate executives to signal credibility to Wall Street.

In ministry technology, **faith leaders do not trust Wall Street or private equity roll-up firms.** Pastors and bishops will only entrust their congregation's sensitive family records, counseling cases, and tithes to an organization that demonstrates **theological integrity, ecclesial humility, and genuine kingdom stewardship.**

ChurchCore establishes the **Ecclesial Advisory Board & Council of Reference**: an independent body of respected leaders who provide spiritual oversight, ethical accountability, and strategic guidance.

---

## 2. Advisory Board Composition & Representative Seats

The Advisory Board consists of 5 permanent representative seats reflecting the core pillars of our ecosystem:

| Seat Title | Representative Mandate | Target Advisor Profile |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Pastoral & Congregational Care** | Represents the daily realities of local church pastors, worship leaders, and family ministry directors. | Respected Senior Pastor of a multi-generational, growing local congregation. |
| **2. Hispanic Theological Education** | Represents the interests of Hispanic Bible Institutes (*Institutos Bíblicos*), bicultural leaders, and Latino/a ministerial formation. | Executive leader or senior faculty associated with **AETH** or the Justo & Catherine González Resource Center. |
| **3. Christian K-12 & Classical Education** | Represents Christian school principals, teachers, and school board trustees. | Veteran Head of School or regional executive from ACSI (Association of Christian Schools International). |
| **4. Pastoral & Clinical Mental Health** | Safeguards the ethical boundary between spiritual soul care and licensed clinical therapy. | Senior Christian psychologist, counselor, or executive from CAPS / AACC. |
| **5. Denominational & Ecclesial Governance** | Represents denominational overseers, diocesan bishops, and presbytery stated clerks. | Denominational executive or bishop with deep experience in safeguarding and ecclesial bylaws. |

---

## 3. Advisory Mandate & Meeting Cadence

1. **Semi-Annual Ethical & Mission Review:** Meets twice annually to review product roadmaps, pricing fairness, data privacy standards, and marketing campaigns to ensure ChurchCore never drifts from its spiritual calling.
2. **Theological Red-Teaming:** Reviews AI pastoral interaction tools (`ai_suggestions`, `ai_interactions`) to ensure automated tools honor orthodox Christian theology without sounding robotic, shallow, or heretical.
3. **Institutional Ambassadorship:** Acts as trusted references for denominational leaders, foundations, and impact investors conducting due diligence on ChurchCore.
