## 1. Competitive Overview

Theological faculty recruitment and academic networking currently rely on generic, secular job platforms:
1. **Generic Higher Ed Job Boards:** HigherEdJobs, Chronicle of Higher Education, AcademicKeys — massive traffic, but 90% of applicants lack confessional or theological fit.
2. **Christian Higher Ed Associations:** ChristianHigherEd.org (CCCU) — basic web job board with no doctrinal compatibility matching or interview workflow.
3. **Academic Society Job Centers:** Evangelical Theological Society (ETS) and Society of Biblical Literature (SBL) annual conference employment centers — temporary, paper-based or basic web forms used once a year in November.

## 3. Detailed Competitor Teardowns

### Competitor 1: HigherEdJobs & The Chronicle of Higher Education
* **Market Position:** Massive generic reach across all North American academia.
* **The Failure for Seminaries:** A seminary advertising for an Old Testament professor receives 150 applicants, but only 4 adhere to the institution's confessional statement (e.g., inerrancy, historical Reformed theology, or Wesleyan holiness). The search committee spends hundreds of wasted hours weeding through secular applicants.
* **FaithFullScholars Advantage:** **Confessional Screening at Ingestion.** Scholars self-select and map their research against institutional confessions. Committees only review doctrinally vetted, qualified scholars.

### Competitor 2: ETS & SBL Conference Employment Desks
* **The Reality:** Hundreds of doctoral graduates and search committees descend on the annual November meeting, cramming into hotel suites for chaotic 20-minute interviews scheduled via email threads.
* **FaithFullScholars Advantage:** A modern digital conference interview command center where candidates schedule slots, upload writing samples, and committee members take unified evaluation notes in real time.
