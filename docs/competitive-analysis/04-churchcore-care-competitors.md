## 1. Competitive Overview

The behavioral health and counseling EHR market is dominated by secular software providers:
1. **The Market Leaders:** SimplePractice and TherapyNotes — robust, HIPAA-compliant tools built for secular mental health clinics.
2. **The Church Pastoral Care Vacuum:** Church pastoral counseling teams historically keep paper folders or notes inside standard ChMS software, creating catastrophic legal liability and confidentiality risks.

**The Problem:** Secular EHRs ignore biblical/faith integration models and church benevolence sponsorship. Meanwhile, standard ChMS tools are completely illegal and dangerous for storing confidential counseling notes.

## 3. Detailed Competitor Teardowns

### Competitor 1: SimplePractice
* **Market Position:** Dominant EHR for private therapy practices and solo practitioners.
* **Key Weaknesses:**
  - Has repeatedly hiked prices while moving basic features behind higher-tier paywalls.
  - No capability for pastoral supervision, spiritual assessment scales, or church ministry referral tracking.
* **ChurchCoreCare Kill Feature:** Purpose-built for Christian counseling centers and church care departments with native benevolence payment sponsorship.

### Competitor 2: General ChMS "Pastoral Notes"
* **Market Position:** Many churches attempt to record counseling situations inside Planning Center People or Breeze notes.
* **The Existential Risk:** If an elder, volunteer, or intern has staff access to the ChMS, they can view sensitive marital counseling notes, suicide risk assessments, or addiction confessions. This violates clergy-penitent privilege and creates massive legal exposure.
* **ChurchCoreCare Kill Feature:** Cryptographic separation. Pastors receive an encrypted one-way referral acknowledgement, but the case file exists exclusively inside ChurchCoreCare's deny-all database.
