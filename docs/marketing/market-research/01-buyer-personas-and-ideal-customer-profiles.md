## Persona 1: Pastor David (The Overwhelmed Senior Pastor)
* **Age:** 46 | **Ministry Experience:** 18 years | **Church Size:** 350 active attenders
* **Psychographics:** Passionate teacher and shepherd; deeply loves his congregation; hates administrative meetings and software spreadsheets.
* **Core Frustrations:**
  - Juggling multiple tools that don't connect.
  - Sunday morning chaos when child check-in lines back up into the foyer.
  - Volunteer burnout and missed follow-ups with first-time visitors.
* **Buying Triggers:**
  - Finding a tool that cuts church expenses while making volunteers happy.
  - A dashboard that shows him who hasn't attended in 3 weeks so he can pastoral-care them before they leave.
* **Winning Pitch Message:** *"ChurchCore takes the administrative weight off your shoulders so you can focus on preaching, prayer, and pastoral care."*

## Persona 2: Sarah Jenkins (The Frustrated Church Administrator / CFO)
* **Age:** 39 | **Background:** Corporate accounting turned ministry administrator | **Church Size:** 750 active attenders
* **Psychographics:** Detail-oriented, financially prudent, systems-minded; guarded against software vendors who overpromise.
* **Core Frustrations:**
  - Paying 7 separate invoices to Planning Center every month that fluctuate unpredictably.
  - Exporting giving CSVs and manually reconciling them with QuickBooks every Monday afternoon.
  - Generating donor contribution tax statements in January taking 40 hours of manual work.
* **Buying Triggers:**
  - Native double-entry fund accounting that eliminates manual journal entries.
  - Predictable, flat monthly pricing that cuts the software line item by 50%.
* **Winning Pitch Message:** *"ChurchCore unites your ChMS with a real double-entry general ledger. Your Monday reconciliations will take 5 minutes, not 5 hours."*

## Persona 3: Pastor Marcos Rodriguez (The Hispanic Bible Institute Director)
* **Age:** 52 | **Context:** Directs a regional *Instituto Bíblico* with 85 students across 3 churches; Member of AETH.
* **Psychographics:** Sacrificial leader, bi-vocational, passionate about raising the next generation of Hispanic pastors; culturally rooted in Hispanic community values.
* **Core Frustrations:**
  - Canvas and Blackboard cost more than his institute's entire annual budget.
  - Teachers manage grades in notebooks; students submit homework via WhatsApp.
  - Preparing for AETH certification renewal requires weeks of gathering physical binders and syllabi.
  - Students study on smartphones with limited data plans.
* **Buying Triggers:**
  - A fully bilingual (Spanish) web platform that works offline on smartphones (PWA).
  - 1-click AETH certification dossiers for $79/mo.
* **Winning Pitch Message:** *"La primera plataforma académica bilingüe diseñada específicamente para la excelencia de nuestros Institutos Bíblicos y la certificación de AETH."*

## Persona 4: Dr. Arthur Evans (The Classical Christian School Headmaster)
* **Age:** 55 | **Institution:** K-12 Christian Academy (320 students)
* **Psychographics:** Classical academic mindset, values discipline and traditional Christian formation; skeptical of secular edtech.
* **Core Frustrations:**
  - FACTS/RenWeb costs his school $14,000/year and teachers constantly complain about the clunky 1990s desktop interface.
  - Secular SIS tools ignore student spiritual discipleship and biblical character growth.
  - High annual software maintenance fees with poor customer support.
* **Buying Triggers:**
  - Modern web UX for teachers taking roll call and entering grades.
  - The "Faculty Shepherd" feature for tracking student prayer needs and character.
  - Slashing annual software costs by $8,000/year.
* **Winning Pitch Message:** *"An SIS engineered for true Christian discipleship and academic rigor—at half the cost of FACTS."*

## Persona 5: Bishop Robert Thomas (The Denominational Regional Overseer)
* **Age:** 61 | **Jurisdiction:** 85 churches in a regional diocese/district
* **Psychographics:** High responsibility for institutional legacy, doctrinal orthodoxy, and legal liability; manages regional conflicts and clergy placement.
* **Core Frustrations:**
  - Skyrocketing church insurance premiums due to lack of verifiable child safeguarding records across parishes.
  - Independent parish autonomy preventing headquarters from knowing if background checks are up to date.
  - Pastoral vacancies taking over a year to fill while churches dwindle.
* **Buying Triggers:**
  - `ChurchCore-Orthos` real-time safeguarding dashboard showing compliance percentages across all 85 parishes.
  - `FaithFullScholars` vetted candidate search pipeline for open pulpits.
  - Pooled digital giving volume saving member churches hundreds of thousands in swipe fees.
* **Winning Pitch Message:** *"ChurchCore gives your headquarters real-time safeguarding oversight and an elite pastoral search pipeline, while providing your local churches with world-class operating tools."*
