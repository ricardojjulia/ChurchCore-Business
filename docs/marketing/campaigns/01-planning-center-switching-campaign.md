# Marketing Campaign 01: "Break Free from the 7-App Tax" (PCO Conquest)

**Campaign Goal:** Drive 250 midsized and large churches to switch from Planning Center Online (PCO) to ChurchCore within 12 months.  
**Target Audience:** Executive Pastors, Church Business Administrators, Senior Pastors running PCO.  
**Key Value Proposition:** Replace 7 separate subscriptions ($600–$1,000/mo) with 1 unified platform ($149–$349/mo), complete with native double-entry ledger and LMS.  
**Channels:** Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Church Executive Search (Google PPC), Direct Mail to Pastors, 1-Click Migration Demos.  
**Status:** CAMPAIGN BLUEPRINT READY

## 1. Campaign Narrative & Angles

### Angle A: The Financial Waste Angle
* **Hook:** *"How many Planning Center apps is your church paying for right now?"*
* **Core Pain:** Churches start with free PCO Giving, then add Services ($49), Check-Ins ($59), People ($79), Registrations ($59), Groups ($49), and Publishing ($99). Soon they are spending $4,000 to $10,000 every year on disconnected tools.
* **The Call to Action:** Calculate your savings with the **ChurchCore Switch Calculator**.

### Angle B: The Accounting Nightmare Angle
* **Hook:** *"Tired of your ChMS failing to balance with your bank account?"*
* **Core Pain:** Planning Center doesn't have an accounting ledger. Staff must export CSVs into QuickBooks, creating phantom balances and reconciliation nightmares.
* **The Call to Action:** Experience **Native Double-Entry Fund Accounting** built directly into your ChMS.

### Angle C: The Zero-Friction Migration Angle
* **Hook:** *"Switch in a 30-minute lunch break. Not 6 months."*
* **Core Pain:** Fear of data loss keeps churches trapped in bad software.
* **The Call to Action:** Upload your PCO export and watch ChurchCore populate your church in under 60 minutes.
