## 1. Executive Philosophy: Eliminating "SaaS Module Fragmentation"

Legacy Church Management Software (ChMS) vendors like Planning Center have adopted a fractured "piecemeal module" pricing strategy:
* People: $0–$99/mo
* Giving: $0–$199/mo + payment processing
* Services: $14–$199/mo
* Check-Ins: $14–$199/mo
* Registrations: $14–$199/mo
* Groups: $14–$99/mo
* Publishing / Mobile App: $14–$99/mo

For an active church of 800 people, the cumulative monthly SaaS bill easily scales past **$550–$900/month**, while still lacking:
1. Native double-entry financial accounting.
2. An LMS for volunteer certification and discipleship.
3. Institutional safeguarding and compliance auditing.
4. Integrated Christian counseling referrals.

**ChurchCore's Commercial Disruptor:** We collapse this entire fragmented stack into **four unified, coherent commercial bundles**, blending predictable SaaS subscription revenue with high-margin fintech digital giving transaction take-rates.
