## 1. Foundational Brand Mandate

ChurchCore’s brand represents more than commercial software; it represents a **covenant of trust** with the global Church, Christian educational institutions, pastoral care ministries, and vulnerable families. 

Our branding governance ensures that every product, sub-brand, marketing campaign, conference presence, and white-label deployment communicates **uncompromising technical excellence, spiritual reverence, and ethical integrity**.

We reject the frivolous, loud, high-pressure marketing tactics common in secular Silicon Valley SaaS. The ChurchCore brand is engineered to feel like the architecture of a great cathedral: **enduring, ordered, elegant, luminous, and trustworthy.**

### Official Naming & Orthography Rules
1. **The Master Brand:** Always written as **ChurchCore** (one word, CamelCase, capitalized `C` and `C`). Never `Church Core`, `churchcore`, or `CHURCHCORE` (except in all-caps legal headers).
2. **Hyphenated Suite Products:** Written with a standard hyphen and CamelCase sub-identifier:
   - `ChurchCore-LMS`
   - `ChurchCore-Academy`
   - `ChurchCore-Orthos`
3. **Compound Suite Products:**
   - `ChurchCoreCare` (single word, CamelCase).
4. **Endorsed Network Brands:**
   - `FaithFullScholars` (two capitalized `F`s, one `S`, CamelCase). Must always display the secondary attribution lockup: *"Powered by ChurchCore"* or *"A ChurchCore Academic Network"*.
   - `FaithCounseling-meet` (hyphenated lowercase `meet`).

## 3. White-Label & Partner Co-Branding Governance

Many churches, Christian school academies, and dioceses desire custom-branded mobile apps, student portals, and giving pages. ChurchCore permits white-label customization subject to strict governance:

### I. The "Powered by ChurchCore" Covenant
* Every white-labeled web portal, parent portal, giving checkout, and student interface **must display the official "Powered by ChurchCore" cryptographic trust badge** in the footer.
* Minimum badge size: Height of 16px; clickable link to `https://www.churchcore.io/security`.
* Purpose: Reassures donors, parents, and church members that their financial data and children's check-in records are protected by enterprise-grade PostgreSQL Row-Level Security.

### II. Denominational & Diocese Co-Branding Standards
* When deployed across a diocese or presbytery (e.g., *"Diocese of Texas Sovereign Church Cloud"*), the diocese logo may take primary placement in the top-left navigation.
* The secondary attribution line must state: *"Sovereign Cloud Infrastructure Powered by ChurchCore"*.

### III. Prohibited White-Label Alterations
* **Security & Privacy Masking:** No client or reseller is permitted to remove, obscure, or alter the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, or Data Sovereignty links.
* **Child Safeguarding Badges:** The ChurchCore-Orthos child safety and verification watermark on check-in badges cannot be removed or replaced with unauthorized third-party marks.

## 4. Confessional Neutrality & Theological Invariants

ChurchCore serves the full, historic breadth of the Christian Church—including Evangelical, Reformed, Baptist, Pentecostal, Anglican, Presbyterian, Lutheran, Methodist, Non-denominational, and Catholic/Orthodox communities.

### The Invariants of Confessional Respect:
1. **Non-Partisan Stewardship:** ChurchCore shall never align itself with sectarian partisanship, secular political campaigns, or internal denominational disputes.
2. **Theological Reverence:** Software notifications, button copy, and automated nudges must respect sacred liturgical language. We never use flippant, irreverent, or trivializing humor in church contexts.
3. **Confessional Self-Determination:** In `FaithFullScholars` and `ChurchCore-LMS`, institutions define their own confessional standards (Westminster, Nicene, BFM 2000, etc.). ChurchCore provides the matching and assessment engine without imposing external theological dogmas.

## 5. Marketing Ethics & Prohibited Commercial Practices

We hold our marketing, advertising, and field sales to an ethical standard that honors Christian stewardship:

| Prohibited Marketing Practice | Rationale & Invariant | ChurchCore Standard |
|---|---|---|
| **Mocking Incumbent Competitors** | Belittling competitors degrades Christian witness. | Focus on structural facts: compare software architecture, transparent pricing, and double-entry ledgers with dignified professionalism. |
| **Fake Scarcity & High-Pressure Tactics** | Manipulation (*"Only 2 seats left! Offer expires midnight!"*) is dishonest. | Transparent, predictable monthly pricing with open 30-day parallel trials. |
| **Vaporware / Slideware Overpromising** | Selling features that do not exist in active code violates our Constitution. | Every commercial claim must trace to active database tables, tested API routes, and verified capabilities in `docs/portfolio/`. |
| **Dark Patterns & Hostage Offboarding** | Hiding the cancellation button or making data export difficult is predatory. | 1-click unencrypted data export (`Export All Records`) available to all administrators at all times. |

## 6. Visual Identity & Trademark Protection

### Logo Lockup & Clearspace
* The primary ChurchCore mark consists of the **Sanctuary Shield Logomark** paired with the **ChurchCore Wordmark**.
* **Clearspace:** A minimum buffer equal to the height of the letter "C" must surround the mark on all sides.
* **Prohibited Modifications:** Never rotate the mark, never stretch or distort proportions, never place the navy logo on dark backgrounds without the white/gold variant, and never add drop-shadows or bevel effects.

### Color Tokens & Accessibility (WCAG AAA)
* **Sanctuary Navy (`#0A192F`):** Authority, foundation, trust.
* **Celestial Gold (`#D4AF37` / `#F3C649`):** Excellence, spiritual honor, accent highlights.
* **Slate Graphite (`#1E293B`):** Modern dark mode surface elevation.
* **Guardian Green (`#10B981`):** Verified RLS security, child safety clearances, balanced ledger states.
* **Contrast Mandate:** All body text on light or dark mode surfaces must meet **WCAG AAA contrast standards** (minimum 7:1 ratio) to ensure accessibility for older church members and visually impaired leaders.
