## 🏛️ Executive Purpose

The **Business Opportunities Engine** is the formalized research, intake, and evaluation pipeline for discovering new commercial avenues, high-margin revenue streams, and strategic partnerships that leverage the **ChurchCore 517-table sovereign architecture**.

It provides an institutional mechanism for autonomous AI agents, research specialists, and human leadership to:
1. Identify high-yield market opportunities and unmet ministry demands.
2. Formulate rigorous **Business Opportunity Proposals (BOPs)** with unit economics and RICE priority scores.
3. Submit proposals to the **6-Seat Governing Council** for formal critique, greenlighting, or roadmap incubation.
