## 2. Head-to-Head Competitive Battlecards

### Battlecard 1: ChurchCore vs. Planning Center Online (PCO)
*Primary Incumbent in Mid-to-Large Evangelical & Modern Churches.*

| Evaluation Vector | Planning Center Online (PCO) | ChurchCore (Complete Suite) | ChurchCore Winning Argument |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pricing Model** | Fractured across 7 separate apps (Giving, People, Services, Check-Ins, Registrations, Groups, Publishing). | **One Unified Subscription** ($49–$699/mo) covering all operational domains. | *"Stop paying 7 separate monthly bills. Get the entire church operating system in one subscription."* |
| **Financial Ledger** | **None.** Requires third-party QuickBooks integration with notorious reconciliation errors. | **Native Double-Entry General Ledger** with fund accounting and automated audit trails. | *"ChurchCore is your ChMS and your CFO ledger in one place. No manual journal entry syncs."* |
| **Learning & Discipleship** | **None.** No course builder, no quiz engines, no volunteer certificates. | **Integrated ChurchCore-LMS** with video streaming, interactive quizzes, rubrics, and PWA offline. | *"Onboard volunteers and disciple your congregation inside your church platform."* |
| **Safeguarding & Governance** | Basic background check integration (Checkr). | **ChurchCore-Orthos** with classroom ratios, elder assemblies, and Gemini AI health audits. | *"Protect your ministry from catastrophic liability with automated safeguarding compliance."* |
| **Switching Wedge** | High friction if done manually. | **Automated PCO CSV & API Migration Bridge** (imports people, households, giving, and songs in 1 click). | *"Migrate your entire church history from Planning Center during a 30-minute lunch break."* |

| Evaluation Vector | Pushpay / CCB | ChurchCore (Complete Suite) | ChurchCore Winning Argument |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ownership & DNA** | Private Equity (Banneker Partners / Genstar). | **User Experience** | Fragmented legacy PHP/Java codebase glued together post-acquisition. Slow, clunky, disparate UIs. | **Next.js 16 + React 19 + TypeScript.** Lightning-fast, mobile-first, fluid glassmorphic UI. | *"Your staff and volunteers will actually love logging in every single day."* |
| **Total Cost of Ownership** | $8,000 – $25,000+ / year locked into rigid multi-year enterprise contracts. | **50% Lower TCO** with transparent monthly pricing and zero punitive cancellation penalties. | *"Slash your software overhead in half while dramatically upgrading your technical capabilities."* |
