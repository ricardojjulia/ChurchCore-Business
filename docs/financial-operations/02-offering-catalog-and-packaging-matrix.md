## 1. The "What to Give Away vs. What to Charge For" Philosophy

A major strategic mistake in vertical software is either:
1. *Charging for everything up front:* Creating massive friction that drives young church plants to Google Sheets or free Planning Center tiers.
2. *Giving away too much without a monetization loop:* Starving the business of cash and creating unsustainable support burdens.

**ChurchCore's Strategic Inversion:** We use **Zero-Cost On-Ramps** where the marginal cost to serve is near zero, monetizing through **embedded payment transaction flows** and **structural enterprise feature gates**.

```

├────────────────────┬────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┤
```

## 2. The "Give-Away" Matrix (Strategic Loss-Leaders & Funnels)

| Offering Given Away Free | Target User | Marginal Cost to Serve | Why We Give It Away (The Strategic Upsell) |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| **Church Plant Seed Tier (0–50 attenders)** | New Church Plants & Micro-Churches | ~$4.50 / mo | **Monetized via 1.0% digital giving processing.** Captures churches at inception; as they grow to 100+ attenders, they automatically convert to paid SaaS ($149/mo). |
| **Scholar Research Profile** | Theological Professors & Ph.D. Students | ~$0.20 / mo | **Builds network liquidity on `FaithFullScholars`.** Seminaries will only pay $5,000/year to search candidate dossiers if top scholars are already in the database. |
| **30-Day Parallel Migration Trial** | Churches considering switching from PCO | One-time automated script | **Eliminates switching fear.** Allows the church to run ChurchCore side-by-side with Planning Center for free until they are 100% confident. |
| **Congregant & Student Mobile Web (PWA)** | Church Members & Academy Students | ~$0.01 / user | **Viral Congregational Adoption.** Members check in, give, and take discipleship courses on their smartphones without paying a cent. |
| **Basic Safeguarding Self-Audit Checklist** | Pastors & Elders | Static asset | **Lead Magnet for `ChurchCore-Orthos`.** Exposes legal blind spots and prompts the elder board to purchase the full automated audit suite. |
