## 2. Break-Even Analysis & Capital Efficiency

* **Fixed Monthly Overhead at Launch (Seed Stage):** ~$45,000 / month.
* **Blended Contribution Margin per Active Growth Church:** ~$523 / month.
* **Break-Even Church Count:**  
  $$\text{Break-Even Tenants} = \frac{\$45,000}{\$523} \approx \mathbf{86\text{ Active Churches}}$$
* **Time to Break-Even:** Reachable within **Month 7 to Month 9** of commercial launch.
* **Capital Efficiency:** Unlike consumer tech or capital-intensive hardware, ChurchCore does not require tens of millions of dollars in venture debt to achieve self-sustaining cash flow. A modest seed round of **$1.5M to $2.5M** funds the entire runway through profitability.

## 3. Cash Flow & Working Capital Dynamics

1. **Upfront Annual Subscriptions (Negative Working Capital Cycle):**
   - Christian schools (`ChurchCore-Academy`) and denominational networks pay **annually in advance** ($4,800 to $35,000/yr).
   - This provides upfront cash collections that self-fund sales and customer onboarding without credit lines.
2. **Weekly Fintech Liquidity Flow:**
   - Digital giving fees (0.85%) are deducted automatically at the moment of payment settlement and disbursed weekly to ChurchCore, providing continuous, liquid working capital.
3. **Re-Investment Priority:**
   - Cash flows from Year 2 ($2.8M EBITDA) are channeled directly into expanding our **Denominational Field Sales Team** and building automated AI features that widen our competitive moat.
