## 2. Deep-Dive Financial Scenario Projections (Year 2 Benchmark)

### Scenario A: Base Model (Steady Organic Growth)
* **Assumptions:** 800 active churches, 160 Christian schools/institutes. 68% digital giving adoption. Blended CAC: $1,250. Logo churn: 3.2%.
* **Financial Outputs:**
  - SaaS Subscription ARR: $1,840,000
  - Fintech Digital Giving Gross Flow: $4,060,000
  - Total Net ARR: **$5,900,000**
  - Blended Gross Margin: **83.5%**
  - EBITDA: **$3,160,000 (53.5%)**

---

### Scenario B: High Fintech Expansion (Bull Case)
* **Context:** Pastors adopt ChurchCore's embedded mobile app giving and "Cover the Fee" prompts aggressively, driving digital giving adoption from 68% to **85%**.
* **Financial Outputs:**
  - SaaS Subscription ARR: $1,840,000
  - Fintech Digital Giving Gross Flow: **$5,680,000** (+$1.62M higher than base)
  - Total Net ARR: **$7,520,000**
  - Gross Margins: **86.2%**
  - EBITDA: **$4,620,000 (61.4%)**
* **Strategic Takeaway:** Increases in digital giving penetration yield near-100% contribution margin without adding a single dollar of customer support or server overhead.

---

### Scenario C: The Counter-Cyclical Recession Hedge (Bear Case in Economy)
* **Context:** National economic recession. Church donations decline by 12% across North America.
* **The Counter-Intuitive ChurchCore Dynamic:**
  - In a recession, churches do *not* cancel their primary church management system (they need it to contact members and collect tithes more than ever).
  - Instead, church finance committees are ordered to **slash software overhead by 40%**.
  - Churches paying $700–$1,200/month for Planning Center + QuickBooks + Teachable actively search for cheaper alternatives.
  - ChurchCore becomes the **#1 Flight-to-Frugality Beneficiary**, experiencing a **35% surge in new customer acquisitions** from churches fleeing high-cost competitors.
* **Financial Outputs:**
  - Fintech flow per church drops by 12% ($3,570k), but net church count expands to **1,050 churches (+31%)**.
  - Total Net ARR: **$6,240,000** (Exceeds Base Case revenue!).
* **Strategic Takeaway:** ChurchCore is structurally **anti-fragile and counter-cyclical**.

---

### Scenario D: The Denominational Sweep (Institutional Network Win)
* **Context:** A major network agreement is signed (e.g., AETH onboard 120 Hispanic Bible institutes, or an Anglican/Presbyterian diocese signs 150 regional churches).
* **Operational Demands:**
  - Rapid influx of 100–150 tenants within a 45-day window.
  - Potential support ticket spikes during initial data migration.
* **The Financial Multiplier:**
  - An instant **$1.1M to $1.8M ARR injection** with a single sales cycle.
  - Drastic reduction in blended Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC drops from $1,250 to under **$350 per church**).
* **The Operational Protocol:** Activate the automated **Batch Concierge Migration Pipeline** (allocating 2 dedicated migration engineers for 30 days to ingest and verify legacy databases in parallel).
