## Slide 2: The Problem: "The SaaS Fragmentation Tax"
* **Headline:** **Churches Are Drowning in Administrative Chaos and Module Bloat.**
* **Core Pain Points:**
  1. *The 5-Module Bill:* Pastors pay $600–$1,200/mo across Planning Center, Pushpay, Teachable, and QuickBooks.
  2. *Data Silos & Security Risks:* Children's emergency contacts don't talk to the school; confidential counseling notes leak across staff.
  3. *Zero Financial Depth:* Legacy tools fail at fund accounting, forcing manual double-data entry.
* **Visual:** Logos of 6 legacy tools with red tangled wires and warning badges showing data leakage.
