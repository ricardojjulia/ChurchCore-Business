# Pitch Archetype 01: The Single Local Church (Plant to Megachurch)

**Target Buyer:** Senior Pastor, Executive Pastor, Church Business Administrator (CBA), Elder Board  
**Target Organization Profile:** Independent, Baptist, Non-denominational, Pentecostal, Reformed, Anglican, or Methodist local congregation (75 to 2,500 active attenders)  
**Tailored Bundle:** [Local Church Complete](file:///Users/rjulia/ChurchCore-Business/docs/business-models/01-pricing-and-packaging-architecture.md) (`ChurchCore v3.5.0` + `ChurchCore-LMS v0.36.1` + Basic Orthos)  
**Classification:** Core Commercial Sales Motion  
**Status:** ACTIVE BLUEPRINT  

---

## 1. Audience Profile & Psychological Context

* **Senior Pastor:** Cares about spiritual growth, attendance health, discipleship momentum, and avoiding church drama or burnout. Dislikes administrative red tape.
* **Executive Pastor / Administrator:** Drowning in tool fatigue. Juggling Planning Center, QuickBooks, Mailchimp, Subsplash, and spreadsheets. Frustrated by reconciliation errors and surprise software bill hikes.
* **Worship & Production Leader:** Needs reliable service planning, chord transposition, song setlists, and frictionless volunteer scheduling.
* **Children's Ministry Director:** Terrified of security mistakes during Sunday morning child check-in and checkout; desperate for fast, reliable barcode/token printing that prevents child custody incidents.
* **Treasurer / Finance Committee:** Demands strict financial transparency, fund accounting integrity (Tithes vs. Building vs. Missions), and low payment processing fees.

## 4. Live Demonstration Flow (The 3-Act Demo)

### Act 1: The Sunday Morning Experience (First 3 Minutes)
1. **Show Child Check-In (CCM):** Search a parent's phone number $\to$ show child allergies and custody alerts $\to$ click 1-button check-in badge generation.
2. **Show Service Planning & Worship:** Open the Sunday service plan $\to$ transpose a worship song from Key of G to Key of D in real time $\to$ show volunteer schedule status.

### Act 2: The Ministry Engine (Minutes 4–7)
1. **Show the Member Health & Family Graph:** Open a family household profile $\to$ demonstrate pastoral visit logs, prayer requests, and AI-driven burnout indicators.
2. **Show Volunteer Training in LMS:** Show how a new Sunday school volunteer is enrolled in "Child Protection & Safety" in `ChurchCore-LMS` and automatically cleared to serve upon passing the quiz.

### Act 3: The Financial Relief (Minutes 8–10)
1. **Show Digital Giving & General Ledger:** Show a live Stripe Connect mobile donation $\to$ show the double-entry journal entry created instantly in the general ledger without touching QuickBooks $\to$ show the fund breakdown (Tithes vs. Missions).

## 5. Objection Handling Playbook for Local Churches

### Objection 1: *"We have been on Planning Center for 5 years; our staff knows it, and switching sounds painful."*
* **Response:** *"We understand completely—nobody wants downtime. That's why we built the ChurchCore 1-Click Migration Bridge. You export your PCO people, giving, and song CSVs, upload them to ChurchCore, and our engine maps your entire church history in under an hour. We even run your ChurchCore trial in parallel with PCO for 30 days so your staff feels 100% confident before switching."*

### Objection 2: *"We already use QuickBooks for accounting; why do we need accounting in our ChMS?"*
* **Response:** *"QuickBooks is built for secular retail businesses, not churches. It doesn't understand fund accounting, donor tax deductibility, or pledge campaigns without complex manual workarounds. In ChurchCore, every dollar given online or dropped in the offering plate instantly balances your chart of accounts with zero reconciliation errors."*

### Objection 3: *"Is our church data secure?"*
* **Response:** *"Every single table in ChurchCore is protected by PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS). Your church's data is cryptographically walled off; no other church, user, or unauthorized staff member can access your records. Furthermore, we enshrine in our Constitution that your data belongs exclusively to your ministry—we will never sell, mine, or advertise on your congregation's information."*
