## 1. Context & Problem Statement
With the establishment of the ChurchCore Executive Governing Council and our constitutional invariants (Branding, RLS, Zero Data Propagation, Accessibility), a formal operational mechanism is required to guarantee that decisions made in `ChurchCore-Business` are actively received, prioritized, and implemented in the individual product repositories.

Without a binding cross-repo synchronization protocol:
1. Product codebases drift away from central architectural and branding invariants.
2. Central leadership has zero visibility into whether directives have actually been implemented and tested.
3. Developers and AI agents start development sessions unaware of recently ratified standards.

---

## 2. Decision & Executive Specifications

1. **Mandatory Pre-Flight Sync Rule:**
   - Every product repository under the ChurchCore organization must include `.agents/rules/council-governance-sync.md` instructing all AI agents to fetch `manifest.json` at the start of every session.
2. **Upstream Acknowledgment Ledger:**
   - Every product repository must maintain `docs/council/ACKNOWLEDGMENTS.md` logging implemented requirements with git commit hashes and verified test surfaces.
3. **Automated Central Reconciliation:**
   - Central compliance is audited and tracked via `scripts/sync-governance.py` updating `docs/governance-sync/COMPLIANCE-MATRIX.md`.
