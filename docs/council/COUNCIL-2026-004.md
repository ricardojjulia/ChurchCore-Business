**Date**: 2026-10-17  
**Status**: RATIFIED & BINDING

---

## 1. Context & Purpose

With six autonomous software applications in active development, central visibility into deployment readiness, Vercel/Supabase infrastructure status, MVP velocity, AI session history, and deployment diagnostics is essential.

This Decision formalizes `ChurchCore-Business` as the **Sovereign Executive Control Plane** for the ChurchCore ecosystem. It establishes a bidirectional protocol:
1. **Inbound Telemetry**: Products independently report deployment status, readiness assessments, MVP progress, AI session duration/model, and deployment mitigations to the Control Plane.
2. **Outbound Directives**: The Control Plane centrally updates development roadmaps, imposes architectural mandates, and sets portfolio sprint goals.
3. **Constitutional Guardrail**: Absolute preservation of Section III: **Zero Cross-Application Customer Data Propagation**.

---

## 2. Binding Directives

### Directive 1: Mandatory Telemetry Publication
Every product repository must maintain `docs/control-plane/telemetry.json` adhering strictly to `telemetry-schema.json`. Every AI coding session (regardless of model or IDE) must update this telemetry file upon concluding development or resolving deployment issues.

### Directive 2: Mandatory Readiness Assessments for Offline Nodes
Any product not fully online in production on Vercel with an active Supabase database must submit an explicit **Readiness Assessment** detailing:
- Current readiness percentage (0-100%).
- Specific technical or environmental blockers.
- Exact sequential actions required to achieve production readiness.

### Directive 3: Central Roadmap & Directive Compliance
When `ChurchCore-Business` publishes new directives in `docs/control-plane/CENTRAL-ROADMAP.md` and `docs/governance-sync/manifest.json`, product repositories must review, incorporate, and acknowledge them within their sprint cycles.

### Directive 4: Strict Prohibition of Customer Data in Telemetry
Telemetry streams must consist solely of engineering metadata, build logs, error traces, and architectural statuses. Under no circumstances may customer, church, member, or financial records be transmitted to the Control Plane.
