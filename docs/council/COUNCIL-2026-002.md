## 1. Context & Problem Statement
As ChurchCore expands across multiple market segments (local churches, Christian K-12 schools, counseling clinics, theological seminaries, and regional dioceses), a clear, consistent, and ethically governed brand standard is required.

Without rigorous branding governance:
1. Sub-product names become fragmented, confusing customers and diluting brand equity.
2. White-label church mobile apps risk obscuring critical data security and privacy attributions.
3. Marketing materials risk drifting into cheap, high-pressure, or irreverent commercial tropes that violate our Christian mission.

---

## 2. Decision & Executive Specifications

1. **Codification of Brand Architecture:**
   - The ecosystem officially adopts the **Endorsed Brand House** architecture:
     - Master Brand: `ChurchCore`
     - Core Suite Products: `ChurchCore` (ChMS), `ChurchCore-LMS`, `ChurchCore-Academy`, `ChurchCoreCare`, `ChurchCore-Orthos`.
     - Endorsed Networks: `FaithFullScholars` (*"Powered by ChurchCore"*), `FaithCounseling-meet`.
2. **Mandatory White-Label Trust Badge:**
   - All church-branded mobile applications, parent portals, and public giving checkouts must include the official *"Powered by ChurchCore"* trust badge linking to security documentation.
3. **Theological & Confessional Neutrality:**
   - ChurchCore commits to serving all orthodox Christian traditions (Evangelical, Reformed, Baptist, Pentecostal, Anglican, Catholic/Orthodox) without sectarian bias or commercialized theological partisanship.
4. **Marketing Ethics Invariants:**
   - Ban on fake scarcity tactics, vaporware feature claims, and competitor mockery. Truth-in-advertising is strictly enforced against active codebase capabilities.

## 4. Amendments Adopted
- **Amendment 1 (Accessibility):** All user interface designs and customer portals must meet **WCAG AAA color contrast standards** to ensure accessibility for older congregants and leaders with visual impairments.
- **Amendment 2 (Code Synchronization):** Any new product or sub-brand proposal must be reviewed by the Governing Council before domain registration or public launch.
