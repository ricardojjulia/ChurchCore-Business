# AI Rule: Branding Governance & Copywriting Standards

When generating documentation, marketing copy, pitch decks, user interface designs, or strategic proposals for **ChurchCore**, you must strictly adhere to the following branding standards:

---

## 1. Official Nomenclature & Orthography
- Always write **ChurchCore** (one word, CamelCase, capitalized `C` and `C`). Never write `Church Core` or `CHURCHCORE`.
- Hyphenated Products: `ChurchCore-LMS`, `ChurchCore-Academy`, `ChurchCore-Orthos`.
- Compound Product: `ChurchCoreCare`.
- Endorsed Academic Network: `FaithFullScholars` (two capitalized `F`s, one `S`, CamelCase). Always include: *"Powered by ChurchCore"*.
- Telehealth Engine: `FaithCounseling-meet`.

---

## 2. Voice & Tone Rules
- **Archetype:** *The Master Architect & Faithful Steward*.
- **Tone:** Authoritative, systems-thinking, mathematically sound, warm, pastorally reverent, and crisp.
- **Forbidden:** No Silicon Valley tech-bro slang (*"disrupting the church"*, *"growth-hacking discipleship"*). No high-pressure marketing manipulation (*"limited time only"*). No irreverent humor regarding sacred liturgical practices.

---

## 3. Truth in Advertising (Constitutional Invariant)
- Never invent capabilities or claim features that do not exist in the codebase dossiers (`docs/portfolio/`).
- Every technical or operational claim must be traceable to active database migrations, API contracts, or verified workflows.

---

## 4. White-Label & Co-Branding Rules
- In any white-label or customer-facing mobile/web UI design, include the mandatory attribution: *"Powered by ChurchCore"* with a link to security documentation.
- Maintain **WCAG AAA contrast standards** across all visual tokens (Sanctuary Navy `#0A192F`, Celestial Gold `#D4AF37`, Slate Graphite `#1E293B`, Guardian Green `#10B981`).
